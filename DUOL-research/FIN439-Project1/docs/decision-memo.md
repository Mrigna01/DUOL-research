# DUOL | Watch / Defer

Valuation date: September 21, 2026. Prepared for a buy-side investment committee with no existing position. USD millions unless per share. Investment horizon: 3-5 years. AI assistance is disclosed in Validation-and-AI-Use.pdf.

## Decision and valuation

I recommend deferring initiation at the dated market price of **$149.42**. My five-year operating DCF gives **$137.57 per share**, with causal low/base/high cases of **$114.20 / $137.57 / $165.01**. Base value is about 7.9% below market; even the favorable operating case offers only about 10.4% upside, against roughly 23.6% downside in the low case. These are conditional estimates, not confidence bounds or a guaranteed floor. The model does not support a sufficiently robust valuation cushion to initiate.

Duolingo's subscriptions and engagement can support growth, but monetization must cover AI delivery costs, product development and stock compensation. I anchor 2026 revenue at $1,207m, gross margin at 71.6% and SBC at 15% of revenue in the Q2 shareholder letter. Later growth reaccelerates to 18% before fading to 10% by 2030: my judgment, not management guidance. SBC remains an economic operating cost rather than an automatic free-cash-flow add-back.

## Model risks I want the committee to weigh

| Named stress (one change) | Value/share |
| --- | --- |
| Growth 13/13/11/9% after 2026 (no reacceleration) | $127.16 |
| Growth at the bookings pace, 11/11/10/9% | $122.63 |
| Beta 1.0 (WACC 8.89%) | $125.56 |
| Terminal ROIC 20% instead of the implied ~101% | $126.01 |

The 2027 reacceleration is my weakest assumption: FY2026 bookings guidance is only +10.9% (Q3 +8.9%), and bookings lead subscription revenue. My regression beta of 0.85 has an R-squared of 0.045 and an approximate 95% normal interval of −0.16 to 1.86, so the 8.28% WACC is imprecise. Ignoring the $206m deferred tax asset is conservative, worth at most about $4 per share. Every stress points lower, so none reverses Watch / Defer; together they move the case toward Do Not Initiate.

## Multiples and disagreement

On the same diluted-share and cash-bridge convention, market EV/FY2025 revenue is **5.99×** versus **5.41×** at DCF value; EV/EBITDA retaining SBC is **41.42×** versus **37.42×**. Equity/reported earnings is **18.30×**, but equity/normalized earnings is **55.13×**. The low reported multiple is driven by the tax benefit. My predeclared peer policy excludes COUR (negative GAAP EPS) and UDMY (merged into COUR in May 2026), so I assert no peer target and do not average methods.

## Monitoring and reversal

I will reassess after the next two reports, refreshing price, cash bridge and guidance. Initiation requires a price at or below the current low case plus evidence supporting the base path. Gross margin below 70.6%, growth 3pp below the path, SBC above 17% of revenue, or FY2027 bookings growth below 13% triggers a rebuild; persistent deterioration changes the action to Do Not Initiate. Failed source, articulation or bridge checks suspend the numerical recommendation.
