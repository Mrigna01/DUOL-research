# Comparable-company policy and multiple definitions

Valuation date: September 21, 2026. Prepared October 16, 2026 from my Lab 08 policy and source record. This document is an AI-assisted consolidation (Codex, gpt-6-astra) of that earlier policy, not a new pre-AI policy.

## Policy before this calculation

Retain the Lab 08 economic criteria: scalable digital-learning operations, recurring learner revenue, limited physical capital, and comparable acquisition/retention economics. A shared education label is insufficient. Require an independently traded security at the valuation date, publicly available financials, consistent currency, enterprise/equity numerators and matching denominator scope. Positive GAAP earnings are required for P/E; negative earnings do not prohibit EV/revenue, but corporate-action and entity-scope problems still do. Do not select peers because their multiples deliver a desired result.

## Required calculated multiples

Calculate DUOL EV/FY2025 revenue and EV/FY2025 EBITDA (GAAP EBIT plus D&A, with SBC retained), and equity value/FY2025 earnings on reported and explicitly normalized bases. Use the same September 21 price and 50.7 million diluted-share convention as DCF. This produces a diluted-equity valuation convention, not an assertion that 50.7 million shares were issued. Use DCF's same estimated excess-cash bridge to convert diluted equity to operating EV. Also display conventional price/reported GAAP EPS separately; its historical weighted-average denominator must not be confused with current dilution.

These target-company multiples quantify the valuation and earnings-quality burden. They are not independently priced peer targets. Comparing market and DCF-implied multiples on identical denominators exposes the gap without mechanically averaging methods. Any final claim of peer corroboration remains unsupported until an admissible peer or reconciled combined-company dataset is supplied.
